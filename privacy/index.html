Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

The short version: Next Reset does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The games, servers, events, time zones and reminder settings you enter are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game images

If you choose a picture for a game, you pick it with the iOS photo picker, which shares only that one picture with the app. The app keeps its own small square copy on your device, without the photo's metadata such as location. The app can't see the rest of your photo library, and nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device. They are off by default, and the app asks for notification permission only when you turn a reminder on. No push notification service or remote server is involved.

Backups

If you choose Export backup, the app creates a file containing your games (including any game images) and events and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes, for example the Files app or iCloud Drive. Importing reads a file you pick. We never see these files.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in, makes no network requests, contains no third-party SDKs, and does not use analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that involves collecting data would also be reflected in the app's App Store privacy details before release.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@gamemath.app
